## 1. Vision
ARQA is an enterprise-grade platform designed to unify operations, intelligence, data, automation, and integration capabilities into a single modular ecosystem.

## 2. Mission
Deliver a secure, scalable, explainable, and well-governed platform that can support long-term product evolution across web, mobile, backend, AI, and data domains.

## 3. Strategic Outcomes
- Reduce fragmentation across business tools and workflows.
- Provide a consistent user and operator experience.
- Establish a reusable platform foundation for future products.
- Enable data-driven decisions with traceable AI support.
- Maintain strong security and compliance posture.

## 4. Product Qualities
ARQA must be:
- modular
- observable
- auditable
- secure
- privacy-preserving
- API-driven
- testable
- maintainable

## 5. Platform Boundaries
ARQA is a platform, not a single-purpose app. It may contain multiple products and services, but each must remain aligned with the shared architecture charter.

## 6. Success Criteria
Success is measured by:
- adoption across target workflows,
- reliability in production,
- low operational overhead,
- high quality of documentation,
- and the ability to extend the platform without destabilizing existing modules.
