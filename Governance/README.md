# Governance

This directory contains documentation related to the governance framework of the ARQA platform. It defines the policies, processes, and structures that ensure the platform's development and operation align with organizational objectives, regulatory requirements, and best practices.

## Purpose

To establish a clear and consistent governance framework for the ARQA platform, ensuring accountability, transparency, and effective decision-making across all phases of its lifecycle.

## Scope

This section covers all aspects of governance pertaining to the ARQA platform, including architectural governance, data governance, security governance, and operational governance.

## Principles

- **Accountability:** Clear assignment of responsibilities for decisions and actions.
- **Transparency:** Open access to governance processes and decisions.
- **Compliance:** Adherence to all relevant laws, regulations, and internal policies.
- **Effectiveness:** Ensuring governance mechanisms contribute to achieving platform objectives.
- **Agility:** Adapting governance to evolving technological landscapes and business needs.
