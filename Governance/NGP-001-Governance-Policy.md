# ARQA Governance Policy (NGP-001)

## Purpose

This document establishes the overarching governance policy for the ARQA platform, providing a framework for consistent decision-making, risk management, and compliance across all aspects of the platform's lifecycle. It ensures that all activities are aligned with the ARQA Enterprise Architecture Framework (NEAF) and the defined Governance Principles.

## Scope

This policy applies to all individuals, teams, and third-party vendors involved in the design, development, deployment, operation, and maintenance of the ARQA platform. It covers all architectural domains, including business, data, application, technology, security, and AI architectures.

## Principles

This policy is underpinned by the [Governance Principles](Governance-Principles.md), emphasizing:

- **Accountability:** Clear assignment of ownership and responsibility for all governance activities.
- **Transparency:** Open communication and documentation of governance decisions and processes.
- **Compliance:** Adherence to all relevant legal, regulatory, and internal organizational requirements.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Regular review and adaptation of governance processes to enhance effectiveness.

## Standards

All governance policies and related documentation adhere to the standards outlined in the main [README.md](../../README.md) of this repository and the [ARQA Enterprise Architecture Framework (NEAF)](NEAF-001-Enterprise-Architecture-Framework.md).

## Design Decisions

- The ARQA Governance Policy will be enforced through a combination of automated checks, manual reviews, and regular audits.
- A dedicated Governance Review Board (GRB) will be established to oversee policy adherence and resolve governance-related issues.
- Policy exceptions will be formally documented, reviewed, and approved by the GRB.

## Best Practices

- Integrate governance policy reviews into the standard project lifecycle.
- Provide training and awareness programs to ensure all stakeholders understand their responsibilities under this policy.
- Utilize tools and automation to monitor compliance and report on governance metrics.

## Risks

- **Policy Overload:** Too many policies leading to confusion and non-compliance.
- **Lack of Enforcement:** Inconsistent application of the policy undermining its effectiveness.
- **Policy Stagnation:** Policies becoming outdated due to rapid changes in technology or business environment.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All new projects and major changes demonstrate adherence to this governance policy.
- A clear process for policy exception management is established and followed.
- Regular reports on policy compliance are generated and reviewed by the GRB.
