# Roadmap

This directory contains the strategic roadmap for the ARQA platform, outlining the planned evolution of its architecture, features, and capabilities over time. It serves as a high-level guide for future development and investment decisions.

## Purpose

To provide a clear, forward-looking view of the ARQA platform's strategic direction and planned evolution. The roadmap communicates key milestones, initiatives, and architectural changes, ensuring alignment among stakeholders and guiding resource allocation.

## Scope

This section covers the multi-year strategic plan for the ARQA platform, including major architectural initiatives, significant feature releases, technology upgrades, and key governance milestones. It focuses on high-level themes and objectives rather than detailed implementation plans.

## Principles

- **Strategic Alignment:** The roadmap must directly support the overall business strategy and long-term vision for the ARQA platform.
- **Flexibility:** The roadmap should be adaptable to changing business priorities, market conditions, and technological advancements.
- **Transparency:** The roadmap should be clearly communicated and accessible to all relevant stakeholders.
- **Value-Driven:** Roadmap initiatives should be prioritized based on their potential to deliver measurable business value.
- **Feasibility:** The roadmap must be realistic and achievable, considering available resources and technical capabilities.

## Standards

All Roadmap documentation adheres to the standards outlined in the main [README.md](../../README.md) of this repository and the [ARQA Enterprise Architecture Framework (NEAF)](../../Governance/NEAF-001-Enterprise-Architecture-Framework.md).

## Design Decisions

- The roadmap will be structured around key architectural themes and business capabilities.
- It will include high-level timelines and dependencies, but avoid overly granular dates to maintain flexibility.
- Regular review cycles will be established to update and refine the roadmap based on progress and evolving priorities.

## Best Practices

- Engage business and technical leadership in the roadmap planning process.
- Communicate roadmap updates frequently to keep stakeholders informed.
- Use the roadmap as a tool for strategic planning and resource allocation, not as a rigid commitment.

## Risks

- **Lack of Buy-in:** Stakeholders may not align with the roadmap, leading to conflicting priorities and resource allocation issues.
- **Over-Commitment:** Promising too much too soon, leading to missed deadlines and stakeholder dissatisfaction.
- **Stagnation:** The roadmap becoming outdated and failing to reflect current strategic needs or technological opportunities.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The roadmap is approved by key business and technical leadership.
- It provides a clear direction for future development and investment in the ARQA platform.
- Stakeholders understand the strategic priorities and planned evolution of the platform.
