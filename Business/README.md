# Business

This directory contains documentation related to the business context, requirements, and strategic alignment of the ARQA platform. It serves as a bridge between business stakeholders and technical teams, ensuring that the platform development is driven by clear business objectives.

## Purpose

To articulate the business vision, goals, and requirements for the ARQA platform, providing a foundational understanding for all architectural and engineering efforts. This ensures that technical solutions directly support and enable business value.

## Scope

This section covers business capabilities, processes, use cases, and non-functional requirements from a business perspective. It also includes strategic alignment with organizational objectives and market analysis relevant to the ARQA platform.

## Principles

- **Business Value Driven:** All features and functionalities must directly contribute to defined business value.
- **User-Centric Design:** Solutions are designed with the end-user experience and needs at the forefront.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Ensure the platform supports and enables the overarching business strategy and objectives.
- **Agility:** Business requirements are captured and managed in a way that supports iterative development and rapid adaptation to change.

## Standards

All business documentation adheres to the standards outlined in the main [README.md](../README.md) of this repository.

## Design Decisions

- Business requirements will be captured using a standardized template to ensure consistency and completeness.
- Regular review cycles with business stakeholders will be established to validate requirements and priorities.
- A clear traceability matrix will link business requirements to architectural components and technical implementations.

## Best Practices

- Engage business stakeholders early and continuously throughout the development lifecycle.
- Prioritize requirements based on business impact and feasibility.
- Use clear, unambiguous language to define business requirements.

## Risks

- **Misalignment:** Technical solutions failing to meet actual business needs due to misinterpretation or incomplete requirements.
- **Scope Creep:** Uncontrolled expansion of business requirements leading to project delays and cost overruns.
- **Lack of Engagement:** Insufficient participation from business stakeholders resulting in suboptimal solutions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- All critical business requirements are clearly documented and approved by relevant business owners.
- Technical solutions can be directly traced back to specific business requirements.
- Business stakeholders confirm that the delivered platform meets their needs and expectations.
